# XCAIC rules

The l'X Collegiate AI Contest (XCAIC) will be on Friday October 23, 10:15-12:15 in room **PC 34**. We suggest you arrive around 10:00 AM to setup.

XCAIC will broadly follow the rules of ICAIC:

- [Contest Rules](https://icaic-official.github.io/docs/contest-rules.html)
- [Technical appendix](https://icaic-official.github.io/docs/technical-appendix.html)

## Eligibility

Each contestant from l'X must meet **at least one** of the following conditions:

- First began post-secondary studies in 2022 or later.
- Was born in 2003 or later.

## XCAIC specificities

- The limit of number of submissions is **15 per task** (like ICAIC)
- Public and private leaderboard will be the same for Task 2
- We can help you if you have problems with the environment, but not if it's an algorithm problem
- **No-cheating rule**: LLM-based assistance is prohibited, use of smartphones is prohibited

### Restricted access to the Internet

- You only have access to <https://kaggle.com> for submitting Task 1, you cannot use it for anything else
- You can browse <https://pytorch.org> / <https://numpy.org> / <https://pandas.pydata.org> / <https://scikit-learn.org> freely

## Tasks

- **Task 1** will be similar to the [Kaggle example task "Ghost of the Machine"](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/ghost-of-machine/), just on a different dataset.
- **Task 2** will be a computer vision task. To practice, [here is a notebook](https://github.com/dataflowr/notebooks/blob/master/HW2/HW2_CAM_Adversarial.ipynb). 

Both tasks will have a **baseline** file, i.e. a skeleton Jupyter notebook that you can fill. Submitting the baseline will not count toward the submission limit.
